About Us

Welcome to JobConnect — your bridge to your dream career. We’re dedicated to helping students and professionals find opportunities that match their skills and passions.

Our Mission

To empower job seekers with real-time access to relevant job postings and provide recruiters a platform to find the right talent — fast and efficiently.

Our Vision

We envision a world where every individual has access to career opportunities that suit their strengths, without being limited by background or location.

Our Story

Founded in 2025 by a passionate group of engineers and students, JobConnect was born out of a need to simplify the job search process. With constant feedback from users like you, we're growing stronger every day.
