FAQs

What is JobNest?

JobNest is an online platform that connects job seekers and recruiters. We provide a seamless experience for candidates to apply for jobs and for employers to find the right talent.

How do I create an account on JobNest?

To create an account, click the "Sign Up" button on the homepage. Fill in your details, choose whether you're a job seeker or a recruiter, and complete the registration process.

How can I apply for a job on JobNest?

After signing in, browse through available job listings based on your skills and preferences. Click on the job you’re interested in, review the details, and click "Apply" to submit your application.
